# Research access and evidence fallback ladder

1. Direct public page: official TikTok Shop seller/storefront, public LIVE/replay page, account biography.
2. Official brand/creator-controlled website, link-in-bio or other social account cross-linking TikTok.
3. Public YouTube live archives, stream listings, public interviews, event pages, company press releases.
4. Search-engine indexed snippets, cached metadata where legally accessible; record `indexed_snippet`, not direct verification.
5. Any permitted, officially documented API/authorized browser tool available in the environment (check terms and limits). Never invent tool access.

Do not bypass login, CAPTCHAs, scraping rate limits, anti-bot measures or site terms. Do not assume TikTok shortlinks reveal final destination. For LinkedIn restrictions, seek profiles and affiliations via company biographies, interviews and public search results. Track `access_status`: `accessible`, `blocked`, `not_found`, `unresolved`.

A missing Instagram count, TikTok viewer count or public email must NOT lower engagement score as though known to be zero. Keep evidence quality and missingness visible alongside commercial scores. No proof of LIVE = not yet a qualified LIVE prospect; maintain in review until supported.
